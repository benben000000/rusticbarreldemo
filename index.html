California craft, properly kept

Seventeen-plus taps running Central and Northern California craft — Russian River, Firestone Walker, Alvarado Street — plus house ciders from 2 Towns. Median 6.2% ABV, which means you can drink all evening without wrecking tomorrow.

Barbecue from the Central Coast

Tri-tip sliced thin and dressed with BBQ sauce, slow-smoked brisket, pork ribs and pulled pork — with smoked mac, barrel beans and crisscut fries. The tri-tip is the thing regulars order first.
