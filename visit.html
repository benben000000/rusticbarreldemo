Before you come

  • Closed Tuesdays. The one exception people trip on.
  • Listed prices are cash. Card adds a processing fee.
  • Come early for a screen seat on a game night.
  • Big-screen TVs; a bit of noise is the point.
  • Kids welcome — there is a Connect Four.
★

Getting the most out of a visit

Start with the Santa Maria tri-tip sandwich — it is the dish the room is known for. Then check the tap list for something Central Coast-adjacent, because most of what we pour comes from within a few hours' drive.

If you are hungry and in a hurry, order ahead and walk in to collect. If you are here to watch something, check what's on.
