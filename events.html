Events & big screens

The bar is built around watching things together. Screens you can see from the back booth, a comedy night every other week, and enough taps to get through nine o’sclock.

Monthly

Comedy nights

Live sets from touring comics, hosted with Laughing Booth Comedy. Free entry, first come for seats, bar open throughout. Doors around 8pm.

Always

Watch parties

Baseball, football, basketball and the big fights. Multiple screens, sightlines from every booth. Come early for a seat; come late for the quiet.

Weekly

Thursday specials

The Chili Cheese Fries special built a reputation. Rotating dish each week while it lasts.

★

Private hire

We take birthdays, work crews and team things. The back room seats a crowd, the kitchen handles trays, and the taps pour whatever you ask for. Call (831) 601-1397 and ask for the manager — it is the same number that reaches the bar.

Large to-go orders are best placed by phone too, so we can have the meat off the smoker when you arrive.
